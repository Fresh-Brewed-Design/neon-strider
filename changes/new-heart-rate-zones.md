- Programs can hold a heart rate zone (first step of #93, engine only, no editor yet): a step with a zone, as a percentage of your maximum heart rate (set with the `heart_rate_max_set` command, 190 bpm until then), trims the belt speed by 0.1 km/h every 20 s, up to 1 km/h either side of the step's speed, to keep the pulse inside it. It only trims a belt that is already running, never starts it, and does nothing without a fresh reading.
